Norex Kids — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 June 2026

Norex Kids (“we”, “us”, “our”) provides a mobile application that helps parents/guardians and childcare/education staff manage children’s profiles, daily reports, developmental milestones, documents, and related communication. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices. It applies to the Norex Kids mobile app and related services.

This app is intended for use by adults (parents, guardians, and authorized staff). Children do not create accounts or use the app directly. Information about children is entered by the adults responsible for them.

Information we collect

We collect the following, only to provide the service:

Account & contact information

  • Name, email address, and (optionally) phone number
  • Account/user identifiers and authentication data (we support email + password and Google Sign-In)

Content you provide

  • Children’s profiles, documents, developmental milestones, and daily reports you create
  • Photos and videos you choose to upload to daily reports

Technical & usage information

  • App interaction/usage analytics (via Firebase Analytics)
  • Crash logs and diagnostics (via Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Device identifiers and push notification tokens (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) to deliver notifications

Payment information

  • We do not collect or store full card details in the app. Card payments are processed by our payment provider, Paystack, through their secure hosted checkout. We store only a tokenized reference to a saved card.

How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you
  • To provide core features (children’s profiles, daily reports, milestones, documents, notifications)
  • To process payments and provide invoices
  • To monitor stability, fix crashes, and improve the app
  • To communicate with you about your account and service

We do not sell your data, and we do not use children’s data for behavioral advertising. The app contains no third-party advertising.

Children’s privacy

Information about children is provided by the responsible adult (parent/guardian or authorized staff). We use children’s information solely to provide the service to that account and do not use it for advertising or profiling. If you believe a child’s information has been provided without proper authorization, contact us at [privacy email] and we will delete it.

How we share information

We share information only with service providers who process it on our behalf and under our instructions:

  • Google Firebase (analytics, crash reporting, push notifications)
  • Google Sign-In (authentication)
  • Paystack (payment processing)

We may also disclose information if required by law. We do not otherwise sell or share your data with third parties for their own purposes.

Data security & retention

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We retain personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. Certain records (e.g. invoices and financial transaction records) may be retained longer where required by law, then deleted.
